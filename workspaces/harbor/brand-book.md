# Harbor brand book

Version 7, for everyone who makes anything with the Harbor name on it.

## Welcome to Harbor

Harbor makes drinkware for people who spend their days outside. We started on a cold shore with one steel bottle and a promise: what you fill at dawn is still cold at dusk. This book is how we talk, how we look, and what we never say.

## How we sound

Calm. Factual. Outdoors. Short words, short sentences. We describe what the product does and we stop there. We do not shout. We do not use exclamation marks. We never promise what the product sheet does not say.

## Words we use

Shore, dawn, dusk, cold, steel, long day, pack, trail, tide. We say 'keeps drinks cold for 24 hours'. We say 'built for long days outside'. We say 'fill it once'.

## Words we never use

Eco-friendly. Green. Sustainable. Guilt-free. BPA-free. Revolutionary. Game-changing. Any claim about recycling, carbon, or certifications. If a claim is not on the product sheet, it is not a claim.

## Colours

Deep Tide (navy, #0f2f4a) is our ground. Foam (white, #f2f5f7) is our light. Kelp (green, #2f6b4f) is for small accents only. Never put Kelp behind text. Never use a gradient.

## Type

Headlines in Archivo Semibold. Body in Source Sans. Headlines are never longer than six words. Subheadlines never longer than twelve. Body text never longer than twenty-five words on a poster.

## The logo

The Harbor wordmark sits bottom-right on every poster with clear space equal to the height of the H on all sides. Never on a photo without the Foam plate behind it. Never rotated. Never smaller than 24 mm on an A3 poster.

## Photography

Real light, real weather. Dawn and dusk. Mist, rain, cold water. The bottle is always in use or just put down, never floating in white space. No people's faces. Hands are fine.

## Pictures and words

The picture never contains words. Headlines, subheadlines and body text are set in type over the picture by the layout, never painted into the image. Image prompts describe the scene only.

## Product facts: Tidal

Tidal is a 750 ml insulated steel bottle. It keeps drinks cold for 24 hours and hot for 12. The body is dishwasher-safe. The lid is hand-wash only, because the seal does not survive dishwasher heat. Launch month is June. Colours at launch: Deep Tide and Foam.

## Product facts: Tidal Mini

Tidal Mini is 500 ml. Cold for 18 hours, hot for 8. Same body and lid rules as Tidal.

## Product facts: Harbor Mug

Harbor Mug is 350 ml with a sliding lid. Cold for 6 hours, hot for 4. Dishwasher-safe, lid included.
