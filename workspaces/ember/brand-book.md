# Ember Coffee Co. brand book

Version 3, for everyone who makes anything with the Ember name on it.

## Who we are

Ember Coffee Co. is a small-batch roaster in Leeds. We roast dark, bold coffee for people who need their mornings to be a little kinder. We sell whole-bean and ground coffee, a subscription, and a short range of brewing kit.

## How we sound

Warm, playful, a little cheeky. Short lines. Talk to one person, not a crowd. Tease the morning, never the reader. Never sound like a bank.

## Words we never use

Cheap. Fair trade. Organic. World's best. We are not certified fair trade or organic, so we never say or imply it. We cannot prove we are the best, so we never say it.

## Colours

Ember Orange (#c2541c) is our spark: headlines and small accents. Roast Brown (#4a2e1f) is our ground. Pictures are warm and low-lit, like early morning light through a kitchen window.

## Pictures and words

The picture never contains words. Copy goes in the spec, never in the image. One cup or one bag per picture, with steam if it is hot.

## Product facts: Night Shift

Night Shift is our darkest roast, in a 250 g bag. Roasted every Monday in small batches. Tasting notes: dark chocolate, orange peel. Best within 30 days of roast. Whole bean, or ground for filter, cafetière or espresso.

## Product facts: First Light

First Light is a medium-dark roast in a 250 g bag. Tasting notes: toffee and red apple. Roasted every Monday.

## Freshness

Every bag carries its roast date. Orders placed by 11:00 on Monday ship that week. Nothing leaves the roastery more than 7 days after roasting.

## Subscription

Coffee every 1, 2 or 4 weeks, roasted the week it ships. Pause or cancel any time. Subscribers save 10% and get free UK delivery.

## Next launch: Slow Burn decaf

Slow Burn is our first decaf, launching in March. Swiss Water process, so no chemicals. Tasting notes: milk chocolate and caramel. It is roasted with the same Monday batches. The launch can be talked about now: posters, posts and pre-orders are all fine.

## Brewing kit warranty

The Stoker kettle and the Kindling grinder have a 2-year warranty. The Hearth pour-over kit has 1 year. Glass and ceramic broken after delivery are not covered.

## Approvals

Every poster is approved by the brand team before it goes out. Allow two working days.
