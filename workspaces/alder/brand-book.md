# Alder brand book

## Who we are

Alder makes camp cookware with alder-wood handles. One pan that goes from the campfire to the table. We sell the Ridge Skillet, a small saucepan and a kettle.

## How we sound

Warm, plain and unhurried. Short sentences. Talk about the meal and the people, not the metal.

## Colours

Moss (#4F6B4A) leads. Slate (#5A6670) for type. Birch (#E9E2D0) for space. Pictures are early evening, warm light, food in the pan.
