# Alder brand book

## Who we are
Alder makes walking boots for people who walk every weekend, not once a year. The Ridgeline is one boot, made well, meant to be resoled rather than replaced.

## Voice
Grounded and plain. We say what the boot does and stop. No superlatives, no exclamation marks.

## Colours
Forest (#2F4A3A) is the main colour. Bone (#EDE7DA) is the background. Nothing else.

## Warranty
Three years on the upper and the stitching. We never call the boot indestructible or waterproof forever; boots wear, and ours are made to be repaired.
