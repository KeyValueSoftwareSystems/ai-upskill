# market_research

Pick me when: finding out what people are talking about, e.g. this season's trends for the brand's audience.
Uses: search_web
Hides: search_web (prefer_over)

## Steps

1. Call run_tool with name "search_web" and a short query about the audience and the season.
2. Read every page that comes back.
3. Give three themes the brand could use, each with the address of the page it came from.
4. Skip anything about other brands' products, and never repeat a claim the brand's never-say list bans.
