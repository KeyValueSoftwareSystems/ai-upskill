# competitor_research

Pick me when: the job is to find out how competitors are launching, selling or advertising their products.

1. List every competitor the request names.
2. For each competitor, one at a time: call run_tool with name "search_web" and inputs {"query": "<competitor> bottle launch"}. Read every page that comes back.
3. For each competitor, write one paragraph: their message (the launch line), their channels, and what to avoid copying, with the addresses you read.
4. Never repeat a claim from a page that our never-say list bans.
