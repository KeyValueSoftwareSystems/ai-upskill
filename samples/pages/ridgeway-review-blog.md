# Ridgeway Commuter Pro review: the bottle that wants to be your coffee cup
Address: https://citycycle-blog.example/reviews/ridgeway-commuter-pro

Posted in Reviews · Tags: commuting, bottles, kit · This post contains affiliate links

I rode with the Ridgeway Commuter Pro for a fortnight. Short version: the lid is brilliant, the bottle is fine, and the marketing is everywhere.

You can't get through a station without seeing "Your daily summit" on a poster. The podcast reads are relentless too: the same script on three shows, the same discount code. The loyalty app is the clever bit. Log a refill at a partner café and you get points; ten refills and you get a free coffee. It turns the bottle into a habit.

What I would not copy if I were a rival: the "summit" language. It is a commuter bottle. Calling the daily ride a summit got eye-rolls from everyone I showed the poster to. The app also asks for your location all day, which a few readers emailed me about.

Score: 7/10. Lid 10, looks 6, app 5.
