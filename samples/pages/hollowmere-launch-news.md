# Hollowmere launches the Heritage Flask with a nod to the past

Hollowmere is selling nostalgia. The Heritage Flask comes in a hammered-steel finish with a cork-lined cap, and the campaign line is "Made like they used to". Print ads in weekend newspapers and two country-living magazines show the flask on a tartan rug next to a vintage car.

The brand has partnered with a heritage railway for a "flask and a ride" ticket bundle, and runs a radio spot on a station aimed at listeners over fifty. Online spend is low; the website still has a 2019 cookie banner.

What works: Hollowmere knows its audience, and the railway bundle sold well. What to avoid copying: "Made like they used to" invites the question "where?" The flask is made in a modern factory overseas, and a trading standards complaint about the wording is open. The cork cap also cannot go in the dishwasher, which the ads do not say.
