# Kestrel bets on colour: twelve shades and a swap shop

Kestrel's spring range comes in twelve colours, each named after a bird, and the launch line is "Pick your flock". Lids and sleeves are sold separately, so buyers can mix them; the brand says a third of buyers bought a second lid within a month.

The campaign runs almost entirely on a photo-sharing app, through a "show your flock" hashtag that reposts customer photos to the brand's account, plus pop-up "swap shops" at three city festivals where people trade lids. There is no TV, no podcast and no outdoor posters.

What works: user photos do the selling, and the swap shops got local press. What to avoid copying: the colours are the whole message. Nothing in the campaign says what the bottle does, and two retail buyers told us returns are high because people expected it to keep drinks cold longer than it does (16 hours).
