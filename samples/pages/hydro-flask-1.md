Role Model and Hydro Flask Launch Limited-Edition Drinkware Collection Inspired by ‘Chuck Timely & The Hourglass’
role model hydroflask collection shop online
Hydro Flask announced a collaboration with singer-songwriter Role Model (Tucker Pilsbury), featuring stylish drinkware inspired by his third studio album, “Chuck Timely & The Hourglass.”
To celebrate its launch, Role Model released a promotional video with his friend and longtime collaborator Ky Newman teasing the limited-edition collection, which is timed with the opening night of Role Model Presents: Chuck On Tour.

Role Model and Hydro Flask Launch Limited Edition Drinkware Collection
Hydro Flask x Role Model
The collection features eight Hydro Flask bottles that were selected and designed in partnership with Role Model, including variations of top-selling bottles such as the 32 oz Wide Flex Straw Cap and the 400 mL Micro Hydro.
This marks the second artist-inspired drop released through the Hydro Flask x Universal Music Group partnership, which aims to promote reusable drinkware throughout the music industry.
“Hydro Flask has always led with joy. Role Model brings that same spirit to music, and our partnership was a natural fit,” said Larry Witt, the president of Hydro Flask. “Since launching our partnership with UMG, we’ve seen a strong response from fans and artists. Our new campaign with Role Model shows that reusables can be part of everyday life, and that making a positive impact can be simple, easy, and even a little fun.”
The full collection is available now on the Hydro Flask website.
