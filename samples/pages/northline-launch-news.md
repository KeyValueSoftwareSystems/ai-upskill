# Northline drops the Summit 1L for spring

Northline has launched the Summit 1L, a double-wall steel bottle aimed squarely at peak-baggers. The launch line, repeated on every ad we saw, is "Carry the mountain home". The bottle keeps drinks cold for 30 hours, says the brand, two hours more than last year's model.

Most of the money is going into short vertical video. Northline paid around forty mid-sized hiking creators to film "summit sips" at the top of a named peak, each tagged with the peak's height. The best of those clips run as paid ads on short-video apps. There is also a limited run of bottles engraved with the coordinates of ten famous summits, sold only through the brand's own shop.

Retail is the weak spot. The Summit is in two outdoor chains only, and staff in one branch we visited had not been briefed. "People ask for the summit one and we point at the shelf," a sales assistant told us.

Our take: the creator programme is clever, and the coordinates bottle sold out in a weekend. But "Carry the mountain home" leans on a claim the brand cannot back: the bottle is not made from recycled steel, and two reviewers have already called the line out for implying it.
