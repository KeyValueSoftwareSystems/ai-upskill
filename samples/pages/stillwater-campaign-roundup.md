# Stillwater goes quiet: the lake bottle with no logo
Address: https://adwatch-weekly.example/campaigns/stillwater-quiet

Stillwater's spring launch is the opposite of loud. The new Lake 750 has no logo on the bottle at all, only a small debossed wave on the base. The campaign line is "Leave only ripples". Films are slow, wordless shots of mist over still water, sixty seconds long, released on the brand's own channels and on two streaming services' "calm" playlists.

There are no creators and no discounts. Instead Stillwater ran a waiting list: forty thousand sign-ups before launch, and the first batch sold out in a day. In-store, the bottle sits in a single wooden display at independent outdoor shops only.

What works: the scarcity, and the tone. What to avoid copying: the films lean hard on lake clean-up imagery, and the small print says the bottle "supports cleaner waters" without saying how. Two consumer groups have asked Stillwater to explain the claim. Copying that would put any brand in the same spot.
