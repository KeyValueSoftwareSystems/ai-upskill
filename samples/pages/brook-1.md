# Brook Summit bottle: built for a lifetime outdoors
Address: https://brook.example/journal/summit-bottle-launch

Brook Outfitters · Journal · Launch news

The new Brook Summit bottle arrives this season in three trail colours: ridge grey, lichen and dusk. It keeps water cold for 30 hours and coffee hot for 14.

Every Summit bottle comes with our lifetime guarantee. If it dents, leaks or loses its seal, we replace it, for life. The lifetime guarantee is the centre of the launch: it leads the hero banner, the product page and every paid social post.

Channels: a hero film on video platforms, trail-running clubs as ambassadors, and refill stations at three national park visitor centres. Email subscribers got early access a week before launch.

What the campaign says: "One bottle. Every trail. A lifetime guarantee." The tone is bold and loud, with exclamation marks and big claims about forever.
