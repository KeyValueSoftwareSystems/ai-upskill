# Press release: Ridgeway launches the Commuter Pro
Address: https://ridgeway-bottles.example/press/commuter-pro

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE · Media contact: press office · Download the press kit (zip, 48 MB)

Ridgeway today launched the Commuter Pro, a 600 ml bottle with a one-hand flip lid, built for "the trail you take every weekday". The campaign line is "Your daily summit". Ridgeway says the bottle fits every major car cup-holder and every train table on the market.

The campaign runs on station posters in six cities, podcast reads on three morning-commute shows and a partnership with a bike-to-work scheme, which gives members a discount code. A loyalty app awards points for every refill logged at partner cafés.

"Most people carry a bottle further on a Tuesday than on a Saturday," said Ridgeway's head of brand. "We designed for the Tuesday."
