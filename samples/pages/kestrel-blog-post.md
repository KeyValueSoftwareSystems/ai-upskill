# I bought three Kestrel bottles so you don't have to

Confession: the Kestrel "Pick your flock" posts got me. I now own a Kingfisher, a Robin and a Wren. They look fantastic lined up on a shelf.

Using them is another story. The Robin lost its cold by mid-afternoon on a July walk. The lid on the Wren cracked when I dropped it on a car park. The swap shop at the festival was fun, though: I traded my cracked lid for a Goldfinch one and a man gave me a sticker.

Their hashtag reposted my photo and I got 200 likes, which is 190 more than usual. I see what they're doing. It's a fashion brand that happens to sell bottles.

Would I buy again? For looks, yes. For a long hike, I'd take my old dented one.
