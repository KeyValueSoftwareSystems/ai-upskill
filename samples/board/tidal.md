# Tidal launch: campaign brief
Brand: Harbor · Owner: Maya

Product. Flask Trail 750 in the new Tidal colourway (navy with sea glass). Launches in six weeks.

Facts we can claim. Keeps drinks cold 24 hours, hot 12. Leak-proof lid, hand-wash only. Dishwasher-safe body. Plastic-free body. 5-year warranty.

Never say. Eco-friendly, sustainable, unbreakable, lifetime guarantee, cold forever, fully dishwasher-safe, or any claim about recycling, carbon or certifications.

Audience. People who hike at the weekend and commute in the week.

Tasks
1. Trends: what are hikers talking about this season? Check forums and outdoor blogs. Give us 3 themes we could use, with sources.
2. Competitors: how are Hydro Flask, Stanley, Yeti, Owala and Klean Kanteen launching bottles this season? One paragraph each: message, channels, what to avoid copying.
3. Every size: the approved poster in all our placements: feed 1080x1080 and 1080x1350, story 1080x1920, web 1200x628, display 300x250.
4. Hand back: attach the sizes to this card and move it to Ready for review.

Deadline. Sizes by Friday.
