# The trail cafe refill map, spring edition
Address: https://trailcafes.example/refill-map-spring

Forty new trail cafes joined the free refill map this spring. Hikers told us the top three things they want:
fill up without queueing, a bottle that keeps water cold all day, and a lid that does not leak in the bag.
Early starts are popular: several cafes now open at 6am for dawn walkers.
